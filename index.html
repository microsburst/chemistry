Data Sources

Element properties (mass, melting and boiling points, density, electron configurations, discoverers, summaries): Bowserinator Periodic-Table-JSON, which draws from Wikipedia.

Chinese names (元素 1–84): 汉典 zdic.net.
Chinese names (元素 85–118): Chinese Academy of Sciences official announcements (113–118 finalized 2017-05-09).

Notes

The Chinese names for super-heavy elements (104, 105, 106, 107, 108, 110, 111, 114, 116) use rare characters from CJK Extension B/E. If they don't render correctly, your device may lack the full CJK font set — the data itself is correct.

Synthetic elements often show "—" for properties that haven't been measured or are unknown. This is intentional rather than fabricated.
